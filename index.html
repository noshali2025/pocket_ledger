Pocket Ledger by Arriverr

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Pocket Ledger keeps the business records you enter or import on your Android device. You can use every workspace offline without an account.

Records you choose to enter

Retail, Restaurant, Pharmacy and Business Management let you prepare records such as products, quantities, prices, medicine batches, purchases, menus and recipes. Water Delivery, Milk and LPG can also hold customer and supplier names, phone numbers, email addresses, delivery addresses, routes, schedules, balances and deposits. The information depends on the workspace and fields you use.

Pocket Ledger uses those records to display, edit, search and export your work. It also remembers which workspace you last chose.

What leaves your device

The app sends no ledger records to Arriverr, advertisers or other recipients. It includes no advertising, usage analytics or crash-reporting service. Typing a delivery address or a phone number in a ledger uses only the text you supply. The app does not access your GPS location or contact list.

Files you import or export

You select each import document through the Android file picker. Pocket Ledger reads the selected file on your device and saves imported records when you confirm. File handling can create temporary copies in the app’s private cache.

When you export a spreadsheet or PDF, you choose where to save it. If you later share it or upload it to an Arriverr platform, the recipient receives that copy and its own privacy practices apply. Pocket Ledger does not automatically sync with those platforms.

Storage and deletion

Saved records and your workspace choice stay in Android’s app-private storage until you remove them. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for Pocket Ledger. Your device’s screen lock helps protect access to the app.

To erase all local records and cached files, open Android Settings, choose Apps, then Pocket Ledger, Storage and Clear storage. Uninstalling Pocket Ledger also removes its private data. Use your file manager or the receiving service to delete original documents and copies you saved or shared outside the app.
